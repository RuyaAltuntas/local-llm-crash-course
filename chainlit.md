# Welcome to ValorCare AI 😊

Hi there — welcome to **ValorCare AI**. I'm here to help **family members, partners, and friends of veterans** who want to better understand and support the veteran in their lives.

You can ask questions about things like a veteran’s **well-being, daily life, communication, emotional support, or common veteran-related topics**. I’ll do my best to explain things clearly and offer supportive guidance you can use in real conversations.

Please note that I **don’t have access to any personal or medical information** about your loved one. I simply provide **general information and supportive suggestions** that may help you better understand what they might be experiencing.

If something seems serious or concerning, it’s always a good idea to **reach out to a healthcare professional or a trusted support resource**.

Feel free to ask anything that’s on your mind.
